Around Pemuteran

A quiet fishing village on Bali's north-west coast, known for calm water and coral.

Menjangan Island

Some of the clearest water in Bali for snorkelling and diving, inside West Bali National Park. Boats leave from Labuhan Lalang, about 25 minutes by car.

Biorock coral reef

A coral restoration project you can snorkel straight from Pemuteran beach, no boat needed.

Pulaki Temple

A sea temple at the foot of the cliffs, 3 km away, with a troop of monkeys living around it.

Sunset on the bay

Calm evenings on Pemuteran beach, with the sun going down over the water and the hills.
